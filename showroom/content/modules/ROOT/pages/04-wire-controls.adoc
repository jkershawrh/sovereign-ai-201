= 4. Wire policy and platform controls

Complete the Deployment fragment so model endpoint, model ID, provider,
hardware identity, and credential arrive only through `secretKeyRef`. An
environment owner creates the named Secret outside source control; learners
never print or retrieve its values.

Complete the Service with a stable namespace-local port. Complete two
NetworkPolicy objects: default-deny the adapter, then admit only the learner and
presentation clients, DNS, and the approved model endpoint CIDR and port.

Configure platform-approved regions and models independently from the request.
The request cannot approve its own destination. Render the chart and inspect
references, selectors, ports, and egress before deployment.
