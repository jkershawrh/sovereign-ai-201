= Build the Governed Inference Boundary
:page-layout: home

In this 75–90 minute construction lab, you author and qualify one governed
inference boundary on Red Hat OpenShift. The boundary constrains an approved
model route, keeps runtime credentials out of learner artifacts, preserves
correlation evidence, and fails closed when policy, input, or dependencies do
not qualify.

You leave with a request contract, client, Secret reference, Service,
NetworkPolicy, five-condition evidence map, human decision, and reclaim receipt.
