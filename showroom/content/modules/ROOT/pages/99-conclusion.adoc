= Conclusion

Northstar began with a business requirement: use AI to accelerate a claims
summary without letting the prompt decide its own identity, region, model, or
authority. You showed the running boundary, learned which controls are
independent, authored and exercised the request, and proved allowed, denied,
malformed, injection, unavailable, evidence, authority, and cleanup behavior.

The honest closing statement remains bounded:

* deterministic policy constrained the tested request;
* a model participated only if the response proved `LIVE`, complete model
  identity, advisory content, and `model_participated: true`;
* redacted evidence supported a named human review without storing the prompt
  or Secret values;
* participant cleanup did not impersonate Launchpad reclaim; and
* certification remains an independent platform gate.

That is the difference between a chatbot that answers and a governed inference
boundary that can be reviewed.
